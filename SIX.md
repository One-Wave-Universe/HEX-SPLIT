# The same six steps everywhere

One cycle. Every floor reads it. There is not a music six and a gate six and a pyramid six.

```
1 hexagon  =  6 cells  =  6 pyramids  =  6 steps
3 mirrored gates  =  6 steps
```

All the same thing.

## 3 gates, mirrored

Three midlines. Three opposite-pyramid pairs. Three physical gates.

Each gate has two readings — the two `1`s of `1(0)1`. That is the mirror. Not a second device.

```
gate 0   P1 | flip | P4        pair 0
gate 1   P2 | flip | P5        pair 1
gate 2   P3 | flip | P6        pair 2
```

3 × 2 = 6 steps.
You do not build six gates. You build three, and you let each one flip.

A tick freezes **one** of the three. The other two stay shut. Walking both sides of the frozen gate is the two Builds around Hold. Loop is coming home on that same gate, or refusing (quit).

Hold sits in the flip of the live gate — the `(0)` of that pair — not in a fourth gate.

Hardware reading (VTC): three Mirror Gates, two orientations. Same object. Software reading: `m4_pair` ∈ {0,1,2}. Same object.

## The cycle

```
Begin → Build → Hold → Build → Break → Loop
```

Hold is the flip zero sitting in the middle of the six — not a seventh slot. Loop returns to Begin on the same hexagon, or up a stacked floor if you earned a stamp.

Stand the cell up: pyramid. Walk the cell: a step. Flip the cell: the same step on the other `1`. That is 12 addresses, still six steps, still three gates.
