# Virtual ground is the ternary solution

```
-(0)+
```

That is the whole trick. Not three powered polarities. Two lives around a reference that is allowed to sit.

## Asymmetry moves. Balance holds.

```
A ≠ -A     write leaves the mid     movement
A = -A     write lands on the mid   hold
```

Movement is a lopsided pair. One `1` is louder. The evaluator sees LEFT or RIGHT. Phase may advance after a stamp.

Hold is a balanced pair. The two `1`s cancel onto virtual ground. `+1 + (-1) → 0`. No `ds`. Stamp is hold. Show stays quiet.

Do not move by powering a third rail. Do not hold by clamping both sides with firmware and calling it balance. Balance is what the mid *reads* when the two lives match.

Opposed rings both loud is not hold. That is two movements fighting — quit, not sit.

## What `(0)` is

- Flip of one cell — connected zero, inside the pair
- Hold — write of `0`, no `ds` — **balance**
- VTC evaluator reference — the spine the two mirror elements are read against
- **Virtual ground** — a mid-rail the circuit *makes*, not the planet, not a third command

`LEFT / STAY / RIGHT` only exist relative to that mid.

If you drive `(0)` as a third hot polarity, you deleted ternary. You built a three-state binary with extra smoke.

## What it is not

- Earth / scope clip / chassis. Do not bolt the probe ground to a +2.5 V virtual mid.
- A fourth gate.
- Field. Field is Dream for one `1`. Ground is the join between the two `1`s.
- A ditch between two pyramids. Zero lives inside one mirrored gate.
