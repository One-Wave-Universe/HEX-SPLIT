# Virtual ground is the ternary solution

```
-(0)+
```

That is the whole trick. Not three powered polarities. Two lives around a reference that is allowed to sit.

## Asymmetry moves. Balance holds.

```
A ≠ -A     write leaves the mid     movement
A = -A     write lands on the mid   hold
```

Movement is a lopsided pair. One `1` is louder. The evaluator sees LEFT or RIGHT. Phase may advance after a stamp.

Hold is a balanced pair. The two `1`s cancel onto virtual ground. `+1 + (-1) → 0`. No `ds`. Stamp is hold. Show stays quiet.

Do not move by powering a third rail. Do not hold by clamping both sides with firmware and calling it balance. Balance is what the mid *reads* when the two lives match.

Opposed rings both loud is not hold. That is two movements fighting — quit, not sit.

## The mid goes back down the middle

`G` is not a ring around the edge. It is a **spine**.

```
        gate 0
           |
gate 2 —- G —- controller
           |
        gate 1
```

Every triad's mid meets the others at the center and that one line runs home to the controller / rail-splitter / stamp. Star. Vascular spine. The hexagon already drew this: centroid out to six cells, mid back in to the centroid, home to whoever is allowed to read `-(0)+`.

Do not daisy-chain `G` gate-to-gate around the perimeter. That path picks up every write as a voltage drop and the last gate sits on a lie.
Do not pour `G` into chassis and hope. Chassis is not the flip.
Do not give each gate its own splitter unless those three mids join at one point before they go home. Three floating mids are three hexagons.

The controller is not Field. Field lists for one `1`. The controller is where the spine is *allowed to be read* for stamp/hold. Current of imbalance travels that center line. Balanced current never boards it.

VTC-F1 is this drawing in copper: three triads, one continuous reference down the middle.

## What `(0)` is

- Flip of one cell — connected zero, inside the pair
- Hold — write of `0`, no `ds` — **balance**
- VTC evaluator reference — the spine the two mirror elements are read against
- **Virtual ground** — a mid-rail the circuit *makes*, not the planet, not a third command

If you drive `(0)` as a third hot polarity, you deleted ternary.

## What it is not

- Earth / scope clip / chassis.
- A fourth gate.
- Field.
- A ditch between two pyramids.
- A ground loop around the hexagon.

## Hardware reading

A virtual mid (TLE2426 / follower / later a stiffer sink-source) sits at the controller end of the spine.
If the mid sags when both sides write, the soul left.
If the mid is stiff at the chip and mushy at gate 2 because you routed around the rim, you split the hexagon.

## Falsify

- `0` implemented as a third driven rail.
- Scope earth shorted onto the virtual mid.
- Mid collapses when `+1` and `-1` meet.
- Phase advances while the pair still reads balanced.
- Gate 2's `G` disagrees with the controller mid because the return walked the edge.
