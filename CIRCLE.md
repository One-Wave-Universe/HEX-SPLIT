# Circle of fifths overlay (E-510 companion)

Parked at the same gate as HEX-SPLIT music: BROWN→YELLOW scaffolding.

## What this adds

`music_clock.py` names the 12 slots.

`circle_of_fifths.py` names the *walk* that visits them.

- Generator +7 (perfect fifth) weaves from pyramid to pyramid.
- Generator +5 (perfect fourth) is the reverse weave.
- +6 (tritone) does **not** leave the pyramid. It is Express ↔ Compress on the same face-pair. That is the Gray sticker flip already in the clock.

So the circle of fifths is not a second cosmology. It is the hex split read as a cyclic group.
